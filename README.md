Project name: Grave Archive.

Premise:
So there is this place, Abyss, not really a new thing in the fiction, but this one is HOPEFULLY new idea.
Abyss is THE bottom of the world, where everything dead/forgotten/extinct end up.

Dead people, animals, animate and inanimate alike.
Extinct, forgotten, whatever.

There are 2 major factions in here, Cultists and Survivors.

Cultists believe there is a way out of Abyss, and they need to do SOMETHING to be free.

Survivors on the otherhand just accepted their new fate.

There is no lore yet, but the idea is, none of this is actually real and this is a game made by the only living being in actual Abyss, that got really bored.



Mechanics:

During the battle, player has following resources to work with:
Energy, that fills out overtime. +2 per turn, I tried +1 per turn, but it was too slow.
Blood, which player can get by stabbing one of their cards on the board.
Bones, which every card drops on death. Some drop more than others, same goes for blood, some bleed more.


Some cards have sigils, but they are not displayed in game currently, because skill issue.

There are some events:

Battle,		regular battle.
Sacrificing,	delete a card from your deck.
Sacrificing, 	but you also get rewarded, ie choose a card out of 3 options.
Finding a card,	you just get a card, without getting rid one of your own.
